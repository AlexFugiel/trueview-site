How We Started

Founded by Brenden Preston and Alex Fugiel, True View started with a simple idea: premium service that respects the home, the client's time, and the craft. What began as detailed exterior glass care turned into a methodical approach — purified water, safe tools, professional finishes — that consistently outperforms the old squeegee routine.

Today, we bring that same precision to neighborhoods across Northwest Arkansas and North Dallas — raising the bar for how clean glass should look and feel.
